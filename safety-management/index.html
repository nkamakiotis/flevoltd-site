Safety management where the stakes are operational

Fifteen years leading safety, security, health, environment and quality for port, oil storage and airport operators in Cyprus, now as an independent consultant: management systems, port facility security, incident investigation and risk assessment.

The parallel field: natural disaster risk

What I do

Management systems

Integrated ISO 9001, 14001 and 45001 systems: design, implementation, internal audit and certification support.

Port facility security

ISPS Code set-up, port facility security assessments and plans, liaison with competent authorities, security systems projects.

Incident investigation

Accident and incident investigation with root cause analysis, so that every contributing factor can be addressed.

Risk assessment

Risk assessments and method statements, HAZOP participation, QRA projects, Seveso safety reports and emergency plans.

Emergency preparedness

Emergency response planning with Fire Service and Civil Defence, oil spill response capacity, industrial firefighting.

Training and inspections

Certified Lifelong Learning Trainer (CyQF/EQF 5); digital facility inspections from site visit to signed report.

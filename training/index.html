Your site, rebuilt in 3D

We model your own plant, terminal or building, so your people train where they actually work.

  • We model your own plant, terminal or building, so your people train where they actually work.
  • A leak, a fire or a spill develops in real time, built from incidents we have investigated.
  • Each trainee has a role. Responders take position upwind, everyone else moves to the assembly point.
  • Every move is replayed from above: what worked, what was late, what was missed.
  • Each trainee is scored against the scenario and the result is documented, ready for inspectors and insurers.

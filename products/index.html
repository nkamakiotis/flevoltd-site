Loss control & insurance products

Underwriting, risk surveys and claims are three points on the same cycle. These platforms connect them, so what a claim reveals sharpens the next survey and the next premium. The result: better-selected risks, losses prevented rather than paid, and decisions you can explain.

Reactive: claims and post-loss → lessons into risk factors → proactive: surveys, underwriting, alerts

UNDERWRITING · WILDFIRE  

PYRODOTUS wildfire risk intelligence

Price and select wildfire risk per property, not per postcode, and warn policyholders before the dangerous days.

Wildfire risk scored property by property, an underwriting decision with conditions the owner can act on, and daily alerts through the fire season. Built for Cyprus and endorsed by the Cyprus Department of Forests.

What you gain

  • Select and price wildfire risk per property, with a confidence index that tells you how far to rely on each score.
  • Conditions before binding that the owner can act on: finding, action, deadline, the evidence you will accept and an estimated cost.
  • Fewer surprises in season. Daily alerts per insured location, from routine monitoring to pre-positioning loss adjusters.
  • Decisions you can explain to a regulator, a reinsurer or a policyholder: every weight visible, every change logged.

How it works

  • Long-term risk. Country, district, community and property levels; 71 property parameters and a printable action report.
  • Daily alert system. Seven-day fire weather outlook per insured location through the Canadian Fire Weather Index chain.
  • Community map. Every postal-code area in Cyprus, for a portfolio-level view.
  • Criteria informed by real fires. The property criteria draw on forensic reconstructions of how homes were actually lost.

LOSS CONTROL · INDUSTRIAL  

Facility risk survey and digital inspection

Know where a facility stands against the law and its own risks, and what each action is worth, the day the survey ends.

From the site visit to the signed report in one platform: a questionnaire shaped by the facility itself, legal references on every point, sealed photo evidence, scoring, report and legal register.

What you gain

  • Compliance you can see. A legal register of every requirement that applies, with the finding next to it.
  • Budget where it counts. The risk level after the urgent actions, and after the short-term ones, shown side by side.
  • Reports in a fraction of the time. The report writes itself from the inspector’s choices on site.
  • Evidence that holds. Photos and notes sealed with place, time and inspector, accepted by insurers and auditors alike.

How it works

  • Questionnaire shaped by the facility. Declare pressure equipment, lifting gear or ATEX zones and only the relevant checks appear.
  • Natural hazard exposure built in. Distance to natural vegetation, flood exposure and neighbouring activities.
  • Four-eyes approval. The report is issued by a different person from the one who wrote it.
  • Works in the field on a phone, encrypted on the device.

CLAIMS · POST-LOSS  

Digital fire history

Know when and how the fire reached each property: the basis for claims decisions and for the next risk assessment.

A forensic reconstruction of a real wildfire, delivered as an interactive report: when the front reached each property, from which direction and under what wind, tied to dated evidence.

What you gain

  • Settle claims on facts. Arrival time and direction of the fire at each property, tied to dated evidence.
  • Lessons that do not fade. Evidence secured while it still exists, before ash, memory and footage disappear.
  • Better risk assessments. The loss mechanisms found feed straight into property and community criteria.
  • Training material for responders and residents, built on a fire they know.

How it works

  • Arrival-time maps in ten-minute steps over 3D terrain.
  • Evidence register of every photo, video, webcam frame and satellite detection used.
  • Fire behaviour analysis of the wind, terrain and fuel mechanisms.
  • Citizen footage as evidence. Residents’ videos and photos become dated milestones of the fire front.

Loss control & insurance products

Underwriting, risk surveys and claims are three points on the same cycle. These platforms connect them: what a claim reveals sharpens the next survey and the next premium.

Reactive: claims and post-loss → lessons into risk factors → proactive: surveys, underwriting, alerts

UNDERWRITING · WILDFIRE  

PYRODOTUS wildfire risk intelligence

Long-term wildfire risk for every insured property, a daily fire weather alert system and community maps. Built for Cyprus and endorsed by the Cyprus Department of Forests.

  • Long-term risk. Country, district, community and property levels; 74 property factors with a confidence index and a printable risk action report.
  • Daily alert system. Seven-day fire weather outlook per insured location, Canadian Fire Weather Index chain.
  • Community map. Settlement-level risk for portfolio accumulation.
  • Weights from real losses. The factors are calibrated on reconstructed fires, not on assumptions.

LOSS CONTROL · INDUSTRIAL  

Facility risk survey and digital inspection

From the site visit to the signed report in one platform: facility profile, external threats, questionnaire with legal references, photo evidence stamped with place, time and inspector, scoring, report and legal register.

  • Risk index and legal compliance scored per section, with critical findings flagged.
  • Evidence you can defend. Photos and notes sealed with place, time and inspector.
  • Natural hazard exposure built in. Distance to natural vegetation, flood exposure and neighbouring activities.
  • Works in the field on a phone, encrypted on the device.

CLAIMS · POST-LOSS  

Digital fire history

A forensic reconstruction of a real wildfire, delivered as an interactive report: when the front reached each property, from which direction and under what wind, tied to dated evidence.

  • Arrival-time maps in ten-minute steps over 3D terrain.
  • Evidence register of every photo, video, webcam frame and satellite detection used.
  • Fire behaviour analysis of the wind, terrain and fuel mechanisms.
  • Feeds underwriting. Every reconstruction sharpens the PYRODOTUS weights.

2023

Lahaina, Hawaii

Different landscape. Identical impact.management failure.

Independent monitoring and data collection on fire spread and evacuation.

From the days after the fire of 8 August 2023, residents’ videos, media footage and police body-camera recordings were geolocated, timed and placed on the map with the police road blocks. Every burned vehicle and boat was mapped, and the burned area was redrawn around the buildings and plots that survived, so that Lahaina could be measured against Mati.

580 haburned area (Mati: 1,248 ha)
102fatalities
2,611burned vehicles and boats mapped
3video sources geolocated and timed

What the data shows

  • The evacuation order covered specific zones. Residents outside them felt safe and did not realise they were under threat.
  • Firefighting stayed engaged far from the leading front, when the resources were needed for the evacuation.
  • Shallow water along the shore restricted rescue from the sea.
  • As at Mati in 2018, people were trapped between the fire and the sea. The lessons had not been learned.

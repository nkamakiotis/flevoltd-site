Privacy & Cookies Notice

How FlEvo Ltd handles personal data, and why this website sets no cookies.

01Who we are

FlEvo Ltd (“FlEvo”, “we”, “us”) is a company registered in the Republic of Cyprus, based in Larnaca. We are the controller of the personal data described in this notice.

Contact for any privacy matter: nka@flevoltd.com, +357 99 693146.

02What we collect

  • Contact form. Your name, company, email address, phone number, the topic you select and your message.
  • Emails and calls. Whatever you choose to send us when you contact us directly.
  • Technical data. When you load a page, our hosting provider and the providers listed below receive your IP address, browser type and the time of the request, as part of normal web traffic.

We do not use analytics, advertising or tracking tools, and we do not build profiles of visitors.

03Why we use it, and on what basis

  • To reply to your enquiry and prepare a proposal: steps taken at your request before entering into a contract, and our legitimate interest in answering business enquiries (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b) and (f)).
  • To keep a record of our business correspondence: our legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f)).
  • To meet legal, tax and accounting obligations where a contract follows (Art. 6(1)(c)).

We do not use your details for marketing unless you ask us to, and we never sell them.

04Who receives it

Only the service providers we need to run this website and our communications:

  • Web3Forms, which delivers contact form messages to our mailbox.
  • Our email provider, which stores our correspondence.
  • GitHub (GitHub Pages), which hosts this website.
  • Cloudflare (cdnjs), which serves the software libraries behind the 3D graphics.
  • Google Fonts, which serves the typefaces on some pages.
  • Esri and Amazon Web Services, which serve the satellite imagery and terrain behind the Pisak reconstruction page, and YouTube, which serves video preview images on that page.

These providers receive technical data when your browser requests their files. We may also disclose data where the law requires it.

05Transfers outside the EU

Some of these providers are based in, or use servers in, the United States. Such transfers rely on the EU–US Data Privacy Framework or on the European Commission’s Standard Contractual Clauses.

06How long we keep it

We keep enquiries for as long as needed to deal with them and for up to two years after our last contact. If a contract follows, we keep the related records for as long as the contract and the applicable statutory periods require.

07Your rights

You can ask us to access, correct or delete your data, to restrict or object to its use, and to receive it in a portable format. Write to nka@flevoltd.com and we will reply within one month.

You can also complain to the Commissioner for Personal Data Protection of the Republic of Cyprus (dataprotection.gov.cy).

08Cookies

This website does not set cookies and does not use analytics, advertising or social media tracking. For that reason it does not show a cookie banner.

The providers listed above may record technical data as described. Links to LinkedIn, YouTube and other sites take you to services with their own privacy and cookie policies.

09Security

The website is served only over an encrypted connection (HTTPS). We restrict access to enquiries to the people who need them to reply.

10Changes to this notice

We may update this notice. The date below shows the latest version.

Engineer, investigator, risk analyst

Mechanical engineer (Aristotle University of Thessaloniki) with postgraduate studies in water resources (National Technical University of Athens). Fifteen years running safety, security and environmental management for port, oil storage and airport operators in Cyprus, including DP World and VTTI.

In July 2018, watching the Mati disaster unfold, I asked why wildfires are not analysed the way industry analyses its incidents.

The reconstructions that followed now run alongside my safety work and feed the products built on them. Same cycle, same discipline: analyse what happened, turn it into risk factors, reduce the risk before the next incident.

FlEvo Ltd is based in Larnaca, Cyprus, and works across Cyprus, Greece and the wider Mediterranean.

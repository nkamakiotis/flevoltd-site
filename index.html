Natural disaster risk

One method, two kinds of incident

The analysis that closes an industrial incident investigation is the same analysis that turns a wildfire into lessons. Each step runs on both sides.

  1. Investigate
    Incident and accident investigationFire spread reconstructed from evidence
  2. Find the causes
    Root cause analysisWind, terrain and fuel mechanisms
  3. Assess the risk
    Risk assessment, QRA, HAZOPProperty and community risk scoring
  4. Reduce it
    Management systems, barriers, trainingMitigation, underwriting, alerts

Engineer, investigator, risk analyst

Mechanical engineer (Aristotle University of Thessaloniki) with postgraduate studies in water resources (National Technical University of Athens). Fifteen years running safety, security and environmental management for port, oil storage and airport operators in Cyprus, including DP World and VTTI.

In July 2018, watching the Mati disaster unfold, I asked why wildfires are not analysed the way industry analyses its incidents. The reconstructions that followed now run alongside my safety work: the same investigation discipline, applied to fires and floods.
